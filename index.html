What we build

Three kinds of clanker. Same operating brain underneath, so the agent that answers your phone knows what the robot on your floor just did.

DIGITAL

Agents that act

Voice and text agents that take calls, qualify leads, book appointments, run inboxes and calendars, and hand off to people only when a person is actually needed.

PHYSICAL

Robots that work

Collaborative arms and mobile units programmed by the same agents, for packing, handling, inspection and the repetitive jobs nobody applies for anymore.

ONE BRAIN

An operating layer for both

Memory, tools, guardrails and a ledger of every action. Agents and robots plan against the same state, so your operation stops being ten disconnected tools.

How we work

Small pilots that pay for themselves, then scale. No slide decks about the future of work.

  1. One job, two weeksWe pick a single workflow you already lose hours or leads on, and ship a working clanker for it.
  2. Measured, not promisedEvery action is logged. You see calls handled, parts moved, minutes saved, and where it fell over.
  3. Humans stay in the loopApproval gates where it matters: money, customers, safety. The machines do the work; you keep the say.
  4. Then we go widerOnce the first job pays, the same brain takes the next one. Digital or physical, same team.
